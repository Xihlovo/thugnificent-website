🎨 Early 2010s Design Notes for Your Figma File
Since you are designing this in Figma, keep these elements in mind to capture that 2010s aesthetic:
    • Fonts: Use Comic Sans or Impact for headings. For body text, use Arial or Times New Roman.
    • Colors: Stick to gold, black, white, and maybe a bright, obnoxious red or blue for accents.
    • Gradients: Use linear gradients on buttons and headers (like a gloss effect).
    • Shadows: Add drop shadows to all images and text boxes.
    • Borders: Use thick, 3D-style borders on images (like a picture frame).
    • Animated GIFs: Use a spinning "Under Construction" sign or a blinking "New!" icon next to the music tab.
